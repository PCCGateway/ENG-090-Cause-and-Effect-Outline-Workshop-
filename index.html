This workshop helps you turn your reading notes and research into a working essay plan. Complete each section in your own words. Your responses save automatically on this device.

Assignment targets built into this workshop: unique title; clear thesis; author/book/main idea in the introduction; at least 3 relevant quotes with citations and follow-up explanation; 2 outside sources; 3+ vocabulary words; conclusion that connects evidence to the main idea; abstract; and Works Cited planning.
